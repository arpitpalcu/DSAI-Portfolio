\# Job Market \& Career Intelligence



\## Overview



This project analyzes Data Science and Analytics job-market data to

identify hiring patterns, in-demand skills, experience requirements,

salary patterns, and career-market signals.



The project combines exploratory data analysis, skill mining,

role-skill analysis, salary analysis, company analysis, and machine

learning into a structured Career Intelligence framework.



\---



\## Business Objective



The objective is to transform raw job-market data into actionable

market intelligence that can help understand:



\- Which roles have strong observed demand

\- Which companies show substantial hiring activity

\- Which skills appear frequently in job listings

\- How salary patterns vary across roles

\- How experience requirements relate to observed salary

\- Which skills are associated with different career paths

\- Whether job-market attributes contain useful information for

&#x20; distinguishing observed salary bands



\---



\## Datasets



Two datasets are analyzed separately:



\### Analytics Jobs



\*\*15,841 job listings\*\*



Key fields include:



\- Experience

\- Job description

\- Job designation

\- Job type

\- Key skills

\- Location

\- Salary



\### Data Science Jobs



\*\*1,602 records\*\*



Key fields include:



\- Company

\- Job title

\- Minimum experience

\- Average salary

\- Minimum salary

\- Maximum salary

\- Number of jobs



The datasets are kept separate because they have different schemas

and represent different levels of job-market information.
